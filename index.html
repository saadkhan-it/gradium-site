Gradium — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

Gradium is a study app for university students. It keeps your semesters, courses, marks, deadlines, timetable and reminders in one place. This page explains, in plain words, what the app stores and what it does not.

What we store

  • Your email address and password — how you sign in. The password is stored scrambled (hashed) by Supabase; nobody, including us, can read it. A 6-digit code is emailed when you sign up, reset your password, change your password or change your email.
  • Your name, program and registration number — you enter these when you set up your profile. We store your registration number to prevent duplicate accounts.
  • What you add to the app — semesters, courses, assessment marks, deadlines, notes and reminder settings.

What stays on your phone

  • Files you attach — photos, PDFs and Word files stay on the phone where you added them. They are not uploaded. On another phone the item appears with a note that the file is not there.
  • Your app lock setting and other per-phone preferences.

What the app asks your phone for

  • Notifications — the only permission the app asks for, so your reminders can arrive. You can refuse it and everything else still works.
  • A file you pick yourself — the app opens a file only when you attach one to an item, and only that file.
  • No ads, and your data is never sold or shared with anyone.
  • Your data is used to show you your own results — nothing else.

Who can see your data

Only you. Your records are tied to your account, and the database refuses any request for another student's data. The app's administrator can see the list of accounts (email, name, program, registration number and the date the account was created) to keep one account per student and to remove an account when the wrong person has taken a registration number.

Where it is stored

Your account and records are stored with Supabase, which hosts the database for this app. Login codes are sent by email through Gmail.

How long we keep it

Until you delete your account. Deleting your account removes your records from the database — see how to delete your account.

Children

Gradium is made for university students and is not intended for children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.

Contact

Questions about your data: gradium.help@gmail.com

Gradium is an independent student project. It is not an official university app.
