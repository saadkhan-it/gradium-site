Gradium — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Gradium is a study app for university students. It keeps your semesters, marks, GPA, deadlines and timetable in one place.

What we keep

  • Your email address and password, so you can sign in. Your password is stored encrypted, and nobody can read it.
  • Your name and registration number, to prevent duplicate accounts.
  • What you add to the app (semesters, marks, deadlines), so it syncs to your account and is safe if you change phones.

No ads. Your data is never sold or shared.

What stays on your phone

  • Files you attach (photos, PDFs) are never uploaded.
  • Notifications are used only for your reminders. You can turn them off and the app still works.
  • Fingerprint or face is used only if you turn on the app lock, and it never leaves your phone.

Deleting your account

Deleting your account deletes all of your data, and copies in our backups are removed within 30 days. See how to delete your account.

Where it is stored

On Supabase, a secure database service. Sign-in codes are sent by email.

Children

Gradium is made for university students and is not intended for children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.

Contact

Questions about your data: gradium.help@gmail.com

Gradium is an independent student project. It is not an official university app.
