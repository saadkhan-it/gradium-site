Download Gradium

How to install

  1. Tap Download Gradium. If Chrome warns about the file, tap Download anyway. Chrome shows this for every app that is not from the Play Store.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If your phone asks, allow installing apps from Chrome.
  3. Tap Install. A new version installs over the old one, and your data stays in your account.

On iPhone?

Use the Gradium web app: open it in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
