Delete your Gradium account

Last updated: 5 October 2026

In the app

  1. Open Gradium.
  2. Go to More → Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your account and login.
  • Everything you added: semesters, courses, marks, deadlines, notes and settings.
  • Your registration number, which becomes free again.

This happens straight away and cannot be undone. Copies in our backups are removed within 30 days. Files you attached were never uploaded; they are removed when you uninstall the app.

Keep a copy first

If you want your results before deleting, open More → Progress and save the transcript PDF.

If you cannot open the app

Email gradium.help@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, and ask for your account to be deleted. It is removed within 7 days.
